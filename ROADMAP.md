# Product Roadmap — MasjidCMS

---

## 🎯 Released Milestones

### v1.0.0-rc1 (Current Release Candidate) — 2026-07-27
- ✅ Core DDD Architecture Foundation & Domain Boundaries
- ✅ RBAC Authorization Engine (Roles & Dynamic Permissions)
- ✅ Fund Accounting System (7 Fund Categories & Syariah Rules)
- ✅ Double-Entry Posting Engine & Reversal Mechanism
- ✅ Read-Only Financial Reporting Engine (Trial Balance, Ledger, Cash Book, Fund Balance, Income Statement)
- ✅ Production Security Hardening (CSRF Token Randomization, Login Rate-Limiting Throttler, Pessimistic Row Locking)

---

## 🔮 Future Milestones

### v1.1.0 (RC2 / Production GA)
- 🔲 Export Capabilities: Export financial reports to PDF & Excel format.
- 🔲 Interactive Admin UI Dashboards: Visual analytics charts for cash flow & fund allocation.
- 🔲 Event Listener Notifications: Automated Email & WhatsApp alerts for approval state transitions.
- 🔲 Dynamic File Upload Pipeline Integration: Profile photo & financial attachment upload support with fail-closed validation.

- 🔲 Full Multi-Tenant SaaS Isolation.
- 🔲 Jamaah Mobile Companion App (iOS & Android).
